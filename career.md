# Career history

This page summarises my career from my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/barry-s-50135113). It is self-reported: it describes the roles I've held and what I was responsible for. The evidence for my software work is in the project repositories on this GitHub account, not here.

I'm moving into software development after 30 years in semiconductor manufacturing, a tightly controlled, high-volume environment. My aim is to combine AI and software skills with that manufacturing experience to help businesses adapt as their industries change.

## Intel Ireland, Leixlip (October 1994 – October 2024)

30 years in semiconductor manufacturing, most of them in leadership roles in high-volume manufacturing (HVM), across the 65nm, 22nm, 14nm and 7nm technologies: chemical vapour deposition (CVD), planarisation (CMP) and ion implantation. I worked on new-technology start-ups and built teams to meet the demands of high-volume manufacturing.

### CMP (Chemical Mechanical Polish) Area Manager (7/14nm) and lead, 7nm start-up equipment qualification programme (August 2019 – October 2024)

- Managed several engineering and technician teams across four shifts in a high-volume CMP operation, responsible for its safety, quality, output and cost metrics.
- Led the equipment qualification programme for the start-up of 7nm manufacturing in Ireland.
- Set clear deliverables (MBOs) for every team member.
- Applied Lean methods, including Kaizen waste reduction and value stream mapping, to improve safety, quality, output and cost.
- Drove a model-based problem-solving approach to quality issues in a 24/7, high-value manufacturing environment.
- Led strategic planning to meet production ramp commitments and tool installation requirements.

### 300mm Implant Group Leader (October 2016 – August 2019)

- Led a team of 8 engineers responsible for the ion implant (Applied Materials) toolset: safety first, then quality, output and cost.
- Completed return-on-investment (ROI) analyses for equipment selection and capital projects.
- Led a multinational team that sold obsolete manufacturing equipment from the 14nm production lines.
